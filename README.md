## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description

The traditional agricultural supply chain involves multiple intermediaries, such as village traders, wholesalers and retailers, between farmers and consumers. Due to these intermediaries, farmers receive lower prices for their agricultural products, while consumers pay higher prices.

Small-scale farmers also face difficulties in identifying suitable buyers, accessing real-time market prices, managing transportation and understanding market demand. The lack of transparent information and direct communication further increases selling costs and agricultural wastage.

Therefore, an intelligent digital platform is needed to connect farmers directly with buyers, provide market price predictions, recommend suitable customers and optimize transportation.

FarmLink AI aims to solve these problems using Artificial Intelligence, digital marketplaces and smart logistics management to improve farmer income, reduce consumer costs and create an efficient agricultural supply chain.

## Proposed Solution

FarmLink AI is an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with customers, wholesalers, retailers and agricultural organizations. It helps farmers sell their products at competitive prices while reducing unnecessary intermediaries.
- Online Farming Marketplace: Farmers can upload crop details, available stock, expected prices and product images.
- Smart Buyer Recommendation: Recommends suitable buyers based on crop type, distance, quantity and price preferences.
- Market Price Prediction: Uses historical agricultural data to estimate future crop prices and demand.
- Group-Based Selling: Enables multiple farmers to combine their agricultural products to fulfil bulk orders.
- Smart Delivery Management: Helps identify suitable transport options and reduce delivery expenses.
- Product Traceability: Displays crop details, farm location, harvesting date and seller information.
- Multilingual Support: Provides a simple interface with regional language options for rural farmers.
Innovation: FarmLink AI combines AI-based buyer recommendations, group selling, market prediction and transportation management to create a transparent agricultural trading system.

## Feasibility and Viability

Technical Feasibility:

The proposed platform uses modern web development frameworks, cloud databases and open-source machine learning tools. These technologies make the system practical and affordable to develop.

Financial Viability:

The platform reduces unnecessary commission expenses and improves direct trading opportunities. Revenue can be generated through small transaction fees, subscription plans and logistics services.

Potential Challenges:
- Lack of technical knowledge among rural farmers.
- Poor internet connectivity in some agricultural areas.
- Changes in crop availability and market prices.
- Difficulties in maintaining product quality during delivery.
- Building confidence between farmers and customers.
Possible Solutions:
- Develop an easy-to-use and mobile-friendly interface.
- Provide regional language support and simple instructions.
- Introduce verified farmer and buyer accounts.
- Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.
- Use AI predictions to improve supply and demand planning.
Scalability:

The system can first be implemented in selected villages and later expanded to different districts and states by connecting more farmers, buyers and agricultural organizations.

## Impact and Benefits

Economic Benefits – Better Farmer Profitability

Farmers can access more buyers, reduce commission charges and receive better prices for their agricultural products.

Social Benefits – Rural Development

Encourages farmers to adopt digital technologies and provides small-scale agricultural producers with wider market access.

Consumer Benefits – Lower Purchase Costs

Customers can purchase fresh agricultural products with better price transparency and information about their origin.

Operational Benefits – Improved Supply Chain

Group selling and optimized transportation help reduce delivery costs and improve agricultural distribution.

Environmental Benefits – Reduced Food Waste

Demand forecasting and better delivery planning can reduce unnecessary transportation, fuel consumption and post-harvest losses.

Long-Term Impact:

FarmLink AI aims to build a reliable agricultural marketplace that supports farmers, benefits consumers and improves the overall food distribution system.
